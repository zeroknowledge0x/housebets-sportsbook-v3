# Product context (inferred from the contest brief; no interview was possible — the build ran as a delegated agent)

- Product: Housebets Sportsbook, a new sports betting section inside the existing Housebets crypto casino shell.
- Visitors: existing Housebets players (crypto-native, USDT balances), on phones first, desktop second, often mid-match.
- Mode: Operate. Success = find a match fast, read live odds at a glance, place a single in two taps, trust what happened.
- Voice: plain, short, sentence case. Errors say what happened and what to do next. No hype copy.
- Constraints: front end only, sample data only, singles only, decimal odds, USDT stakes.
- Out of scope: multis/accas/bet builder, boosts/free bets/promos, outrights/specials, stats widgets/match trackers, AI search.
- Variant 3 subject: Calls. A player posts a single as a public Call (pick, price at post time, live score, display name, one AI-written line). Others take it onto their own slip. Attribution is the name and a take-count only: no percentage cut, no payouts, no promos.
- AI jobs: write the line, mark calls stale or locked with a plain reason, rank the Calls board, group identical picks, explain slip states. Search stays normal search.
